# Gameplay research and next units (2026-10-24)

Source: web research on SimCity 4/2013, Cities: Skylines 1/2, RCT2/Planet Coaster, Frostpunk 1/2, Anno 1800, Factorio, Against the Storm, Banished, Townscaper/Dorfromantik/Islanders, Mini Metro/Motorways, Pocket City 1/2, TheoTown, Workers & Resources, Timberborn, thumb-zone research. Search summaries only (fetch was blocked), so treat quoted numbers as approximate.

## Gaps found
1. Onboarding ends at the six-step checklist; no goal after it. The signature mechanic (robots) was gated behind an unexplained depot.
2. No goals or pressure beyond population tiers.
3. Legibility: RCI bars and overlays say what, never why. No citizen/bot voices, placement score, or jump-to-problem. (CS2's main failure.)
4. Mid-game: robots haul generic "material", so hauling never forces a decision.
5. Late game: only megaprojects; pressure fades (Banished).
6. Mobile UX: HUD heavy, no long-press radial, no one-handed layout, no "while you were away".
7. Failure states never change player behaviour.
8. Little player expression (districts, names, bot paint).

## Proposals (priority order)
P1 Foreman directive chain (UNIT-0) replacing the checklist, rewards via sim command (Factorio FFF #241, Pocket City quests).
P2 Starter depot + stub road pre-placed.
P3 UNIT-0 advisor: top issues ranked, tap to jump + overlay; silly headlines when healthy (SimCity 4).
P4 Building mood icons + tap thoughts for buildings and bots (RCT, GlassBox).
P5 Placement score chips on ghost: +land value / -pollution / +coverage (Islanders).
P6 Robot research tree "Firmware": logistics / construction / civic (Pocket City 2, CS2 trees).
P7 Bot specialisations + depot modules (Workers & Resources, without micromanagement).
P8 Depot work radius visible (Factorio roboports).
P9 Light production chains: Ore -> Alloy -> Components hauled by robots (Anno, Factorio). Max 3 resources.
P10 Zone needs for level-up (Anno).
P11 Edicts with visible trade-offs, max 4 active (Frostpunk laws, CS policies).
P12 Named districts with per-district edict and style (CS districts).
P13 Approval meter + council requests with deadlines (Frostpunk 2, Against the Storm, SC4).
P14 Celebrations and festivals (Pocket City block parties).
P15 Scenarios with 1-3 stars (RCT2).
P16 Daily/weekly challenge seeds (Mini Motorways).
P17 Telegraphed seasonal hazards (Timberborn).
P18 Disaster gentle mode + Rebuild all (Pocket City).
P19 Long-press build radial + one-handed layout (thumb-zone research, TheoTown complaints).
P20 Minimal HUD focus mode (Mini Metro).
P21 "While you were away" card + safe stopping points.
P22 Named bots and depot paint.
P23 Blueprint queue with bot priority pins (Factorio).
